How it works

From filament to freedom.

Every hand starts in our lab and ends in the hands of someone whose life it changes.

01

Design

We tailor an open-source design around each recipient's measurements, needs, and personality.

02

Print

Durable components are 3D printed with precision, then carefully inspected for strength and finish.

03

Assemble

Our volunteers fit, fasten, and test every piece to create a comfortable, functional hand.

04

Deliver

Each hand reaches its recipient completely free, with follow-up support whenever adjustments are needed.

Why we build

Technology should open doors—not raise barriers.

Traditional prosthetics can be out of reach for too many families. Our team of Ohio high school students turns accessible 3D-printing technology into practical, personalized hands.

Every color choice, fitting, and follow-up starts with the person who will wear it. The result is more than a device: it is a tool for independence.
