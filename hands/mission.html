How it started

One print became a bigger purpose.

Hands Across Borders was founded in November 2025 by a team of high school students who saw 3D printing as a practical way to lower the barriers surrounding prosthetic care.

Today, we focus on people with limb differences below the wrist. We adapt proven models to each recipient, listen closely to their feedback, and keep improving every hand we make.
